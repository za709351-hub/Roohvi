Roohvi روحوی

A voice-first AI wellness companion for the desktop. It listens in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English, offers one small coping step, and shows the right local helpline within a second when someone may be in danger. Hackathon submission · working prototype. This page is the hosted demo; the PC app is the product.

You stay in control

Tracking off by default, data on your device.

Built safety-first, in the user's own language

Every design choice starts from safety, honesty about being an AI, and privacy by default.

🎙️

Voice-first, 3 languages

Real-time conversation in Urdu, Roman Urdu and English, with a respectful “aap” tone and typed chat alongside.

🛡️

Two-layer safety screen

An instant offline regex screen plus a semantic check. At elevated or high concern a support card appears at once.

🆘

“Need help now?”

Always visible. Opens Pakistan-first emergency and crisis lines in one tap, with no model round-trip.

📞

Local helplines

Rescue 1122, Police 15, Umang, Rozan, Taskeen and 1099, each with a source and last-verified note.

🌬️

Calming activities

Eight guided practices including a breathing circle (in 4, hold 1, out 6) that works offline.

📈

Check-in & weekly look-back

Self-reported mood, energy, stress and sleep. Plain averages, never a score or a medical measure.

📚

Sourced knowledge

86 reviewed entries with an official source. No diagnosis, no medication advice, no invented facts.

🔒

Private by default

Tracking is off until you turn it on, data stays on your device, and you can export or delete it any time.

How it is built

One desktop application. Voice goes to Gemini Live; everything else runs on the user's machine.

Python 3.11 – 3.13

Application, safety layer, tools and knowledge search.

PyQt6

Desktop interface, with a three.js 3D character in a web view.

Gemini Live

Real-time voice conversation, with automatic reconnect that keeps context.

Gemini text model

The short semantic safety screen call.

8 wellness tools

Coping activity, mood check-in, support resources, knowledge search, region, wellness data and summary, Nazar.

86 reviewed entries

Coping 30, safety guidance 30 (assistant only), psychoeducation 10, professional help 10, language and culture 6.

Safety before helpfulness

Every typed or spoken message is screened by two layers before and while Roohvi replies. The same rules are also written into the system prompt.

LOW

Everyday stress

Warm and practical. One optional activity when it fits.

ELEVATED

Hopelessness

Support card at once. Roohvi slows down, asks one gentle direct question, and encourages a trusted person and a professional.

HIGH

Intent, plan or timing

Support card at once. Roohvi says it is an AI that cannot keep you safe, urges emergency services now, and stays present.

No diagnosisNo medication adviceNo self-harm methods, in any framingNo secrecy or safety guaranteesMemory guard on long-term memory

Private by default

You stay in control of what is kept.

✅

Consent first

A first-run notice must be accepted before the assistant starts.

🔕

Tracking off

Nothing about check-ins is saved until you switch tracking on.

💻

Data on your device

Stored locally. Only aggregate safety counters are kept, never message text.

🗑️

Export or delete

See what is kept, export it, or delete everything after an on-screen confirmation.

📷

Nazar camera is optional

Off at every start. One still photo only when you ask, shown to you, never saved, auto-off after 30 minutes.

☁️

Honest about the cloud

Speech and text go to Google's Gemini API. A free key may be used by Google to improve products; a billing-enabled key is more private.

Where it stands and what comes next

Roohvi is a working prototype, not ready for the public. Honest limits first.

Known limits (PRD, 4 Oct 2026)

  • The regex screen passes the team's own test sets but only 11 of 24 fresh lines; the semantic layer has been tested with a fake model only.
  • No clinical or professional review yet; 16 review findings are open.
  • Pakistan helplines were checked on 3–4 Oct 2026; other regions were not re-checked.
  • No age gate yet. Local files are plain JSON. The base engine licence (CC BY-NC 4.0) does not allow commercial use.
